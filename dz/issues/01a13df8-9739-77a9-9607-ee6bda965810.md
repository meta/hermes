A GetById site whose only entry was recorded from a legacy single-shape specialization at compile time, and which never misses, keeps `seen == 0`. The demotion pass skips unseen sites, so its helper slot stays `_jit_get_by_id` forever, `JitConsumerRecords::recordingSites` never reaches 0, and every threshold crossing of that version walks all of its GetById sites. Cheap per crossing, but the constant-time gate the counter exists for never engages for most functions. Options: exclude such sites from recordingSites, or treat "never called" sites as demotable once a version has crossed N times. (From the final review of the polymorphic GetById tier.)
