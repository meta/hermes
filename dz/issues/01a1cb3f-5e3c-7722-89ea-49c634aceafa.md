On relay-feed-wrapperized (~/work/react-benchmarks), the x86-64 JIT at
the head of x86-jit (static_h a096ecfee plus "JIT: box doubles inline in
the HV32 and BOXED store tiers") is 4.8% slower under HV32 than the JIT
at a5e8cc44f, the last commit of the x86-64 JIT landing before the
performance work. Under HV64 it is 2.0% slower. Recompilation causes
most of it: relay-feed runs ~0.25 s, and the recompiles do not pay for
themselves in that time.

x86-64 Release, HERMESVM_ALLOW_JIT=2, from each build's own hermesc -O
bytecode, taskset -c 2, 10 reps in rotated order, medians (2026-11-11).
Scores relative to the base JIT:

| | base JIT | head JIT | head, -Xjit-max-recompiles=0 |
|---|---|---|---|
| HV64 | 1.000 | 0.993 | 1.009 |
| HV32 | 1.000 | 0.952 | 0.983 |

In the HV32 runs every head rep scored below every base rep. The
interpreter is unchanged between the two commits under HV32 (0.998),
so the cause is in the JIT. Under HV64 the interpreter itself dropped
1.5%, so most of the HV64 JIT drop is not the JIT's. Head counters, one
run: NumRecompileChecks 38207, NumRecompiles 32, NumByIdDemotions 85,
NumByValDemotions 49.

perf, 20 runs per binary, HV32, samples (head - base): total
23975 -> 25238 (+5.3%):

- JIT code: 8965 -> 10507 (+1542).
- JIT compiler (asmjit Assembler::_emit, label binding,
  compileCodeBlockImpl, syncAllFRTempExcept, getByIdImpl, malloc/free):
  about +650, roughly double the base.
- JITContext::considerRecompile: 0 -> 231 (~1% of the run). Every
  threshold crossing (-Xjit-recompile-threshold, default 64 declines)
  walks all ByVal and ById sites of the function for demotion before
  the budget early-out (lib/VM/JIT/JitCompiler.cpp, considerRecompile),
  and relay-feed crosses 38K times.
- Helpers fell: _jit_put_by_id 1738 -> 868,
  setNamedSlotValueIndirectUnsafe 399 -> 51, and about -500 across the
  equality, get_by_val, putByVal and _setOwnIndexedImpl helpers.

With recompilation off, 1.7% of the HV32 loss remains. The inline paths
roughly break even on this benchmark: the helpers lost about 1700
samples and the JIT code gained about 1540. These profiles were taken
without -Xperf-prof, so the JIT-code time is not attributed to functions
or tiers; -Xperf-prof itself costs about 13% (dz 01a16b01).

The same comparison on the other React benchmarks: draw +7.7% HV64 /
+5.6% HV32, pixel-grid +11% / +8%, pixel-grid-small +2-4%. Geomean over
the four: +5.5% HV64, +2.9% HV32.

Directions, not evaluated:
- Make considerRecompile cheaper: skip the per-site demotion walk on
  most crossings, or bound its cost.
- Make recompiles rarer or later for short runs: higher threshold,
  smaller budget, or more evidence before the first recompile.
- Re-profile with -Xperf-prof (jitdump, `perf inject --jit`) to
  attribute the remaining 1.7% to functions and versions.

Scratch data: /tmp/claude-1000/react-bench/ (scripts, scores.csv,
prof/, norecomp/), may be cleaned.
