The young generation is one heap segment (HERMESVM_LOG_HEAP_SEGMENT_SIZE,
default 22 = 4 MB). On the React benchmarks in ~/work/react-benchmarks a
16 MB young generation changes the score by +14.6% (pixel-grid-small),
+3.0% (draw) and -6.4% (relay-feed). Nothing has been changed; this
records the measurement so the work can be picked up later.

Measurement (2026-11-11): x86-64 Release, HV64, HERMESVM_ALLOW_JIT=2,
-Xjit, x86-jit at eeacdf640, bytecode from that build's hermesc -O,
taskset -c 2, 7 reps alternating, medians. The 16 MB build differs only
in -DHERMESVM_LOG_HEAP_SEGMENT_SIZE=24.

| benchmark        | 4 MB   | 16 MB  | ratio | collections | GC time (s)    |
|------------------|--------|--------|-------|-------------|----------------|
| pixel-grid-small | 193768 | 222098 | 1.146 | 271 -> 68   | 0.297 -> 0.102 |
| draw             | 40818  | 42041  | 1.030 | 110 -> 28   | 0.021 -> 0.008 |
| relay-feed       | 3610   | 3378   | 0.936 | 37 -> 26    | 0.051 -> 0.054 |

pixel-grid-small allocates 900 MB in 2.05 s with a small live set, so
young-gen collections are 11% of its samples; total run time drops from
2.05 s to 1.80 s. relay-feed allocates 132 MB with 61 MB live at the
peak in 0.29 s; its GC time does not drop and the cause of the
slowdown is not known.

Limits of the experiment:

- The segment size is a blunt proxy. It also changes old-gen segment
  size, card table extent and large-allocation thresholds. The real
  change is a young-gen size decoupled from the segment size, or one
  that adapts.
- Only HV64 and only three benchmarks. Full-size pixel-grid and HV32
  were not measured.
- Runs were pinned to one core, so the background Hades thread competes
  with the mutator; with two cores relay-feed and pixel-grid-small
  score about 4% higher at 4 MB.
- A larger young generation costs memory, and longer young-gen pauses,
  which matter on mobile.

To do before designing anything: sweep young-gen sizes (8, 16, 32 MB),
find out why relay-feed regresses (page faults on first touch of the
larger nursery and the old-gen collection trigger are the suspects, and
neither is verified), and repeat on HV32 and full-size pixel-grid.
