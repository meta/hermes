When a GetById site keeps seeing hidden classes that die (short-lived shapes), the recorder overwrites dead entries and sets `changed` each time. The demotion rule resets stability on `changed`, so such a site may never demote and keeps paying the recording helper's cost indefinitely, even after the recompile budget is exhausted and nothing can act on what it learns. Candidate fix: ignore `changed` for ById sites when no recompile is possible (`!actionablePossible`), as the rule already does for progress. (From the final review of the polymorphic GetById tier.)
