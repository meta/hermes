After GetById chain entries landed on x86-jit, a fixed-work DeltaBlue
run (`doDeterministic: true`, 4400 iterations per phase,
`cmake-build-jit2`, `-Xjit`, counted with temporary counters in
`getByIdWithReceiver_RJS`, the dz 970b instrumentation) still makes
21.1M slow GetById lookups (base: 285.4M). Of those:

- 12.76M are `prototype` reads from `_sh_ljs_create_this` (`new`), not
  GetById sites; base has exactly the same count.
- 8.34M go through `_jit_get_by_id` from 64 sites that are still
  recording. 99% of these calls come from sites that saw 3 or 4
  receiver classes, and 89% find the property at depth 2 or more.
- 36K go through the plain helper.

Why those 64 sites keep missing was not investigated. Candidate
causes:

- The recompile budget (2 per function) is exhausted while the site
  still holds live chain entries that were recorded but never emitted,
  so they are refreshed but every call still forwards and counts.
- The four-entry cap (`JitByIdSiteRecord::kMaxEntries`) overflows: a
  site that sees own or prototype shapes plus 3-4 chain receiver
  classes goes megamorphic and drops the rest.
- Entries are emitted but keep missing their epoch guard and are
  refreshed or rejected in the helper (`NumByIdChainRefreshes` was 0
  in the counter run, which argues against this).

A first step: per-site counts of entries by kind, `emitted`,
`unemittable`, `megamorphic` and the remaining budget at the hottest
of the 64 sites.

Numbers from the "Benchmarks" part of the Delivered section of
doc/superpowers/specs/2026-10-24-jit-getbyid-chain-design.md.
