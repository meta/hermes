Unproven doubt from IR type system v2 Phases 4-5: lowering of recursive
Flow types (lazy reservation, maps keyed by Flow's co-inductive equality,
`FlowTypeStructuralInfo` in lib/IRGen) may be order-dependent: which of two
Flow-equal types is lowered first could decide whether they share a
reserved entry or get different ids. Distinct ids are sound but could fail
a strict `StoreFrameInst` check. Needs a test that lowers two Flow-equal
recursive types in both orders.
