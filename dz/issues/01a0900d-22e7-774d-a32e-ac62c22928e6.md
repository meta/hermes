Pointer-flip demotion (dz 01a083c3's I1) leaves a permanently
declining ByVal site with a fixed +13 instruction / +2 cycle per-call
tax versus the pre-feature direct call: the failed inline guard chain
plus the indirect call through the site's helper slot. Measured on
the GetByVal controls: f16-load 0.94x, arr-holes 0.96x vs pre-feature
(7% of the accumulating f16-load benchmark, 19% of a read-only
isolation probe, 2.7% of the expensive arr-holes loop). Demotion
itself works; recording covered only 0.2% of calls.

Follow-up: demote by PATCHING the emitted site to a direct call/jump
to the plain helper (W^X flip, patch the movabs+call sequence or an
entry stub) instead of -- or in addition to -- flipping the slot.
Removes the entire residual tax; patching a jump over the tiers
would also remove the guard-chain half. Interacts with the
retirement sweep and concurrent execution of the patched page;
design needed. Applies to put and get sites alike.
