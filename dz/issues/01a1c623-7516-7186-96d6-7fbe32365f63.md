const c: number[] = new Array<number>();
    c.push(1);
    print(c.length);

Flow types `new Array<number>()` as `Array<number>`, but
`emitTypedClassAllocation` emits `AllocTypedObjectInst` (a plain typed
object). `c.push(1)` then aborts in `cast<FastArray>` at runtime (ASan
Debug: "cast<Ty>() argument of incompatible type!"), at -O0 and -O. Also on
the base master commit (3f124a87e23c).
