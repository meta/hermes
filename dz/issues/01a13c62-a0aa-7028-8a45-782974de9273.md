Which untyped benchmarks to optimize the JIT against, and where their time
goes. Measured 2026-10-14 at dd0da8050.

## Recommendation

Target **DeltaBlue**, **TypeScript** and **RayTrace** (Octane), with
MiniReact as a cross-check for React-shaped code.

- DeltaBlue: class-style OO, polymorphic method dispatch on prototypes.
  ~31% of cycles are getById misses falling into the runtime.
- TypeScript: the only large real program (a compiler). Strings,
  closures, polymorphic AST nodes, allocation, GC; no single hot loop.
- RayTrace: constructor-heavy allocation. ~14% of cycles are
  property-adding stores (`_jit_put_by_id` + `addNewOwnPropertyInSlot`),
  plus `apply(arguments)` from its `Class.create` idiom.

Excluded: Richards (77% already in JIT code; the one hotspot is the `==`
helper, a point fix). Navier-Stokes, Crypto, Mandreel, zlib, Gameboy
(numeric or compiled C/C++). Earley-Boyer (machine-generated Scheme).
Splay (GC-bound), RegExp (regex-engine-bound). MiniReact and widgets
(dominated by builtins: StringBuilder, array methods).

Cross-cutting levers, in order:
1. getById misses (`getNamedDescriptorUnsafe`, `getByIdWithReceiver_RJS`,
   `getNamedWithReceiver_RJS`): top cost in DeltaBlue, RayTrace,
   TypeScript, Earley-Boyer, Box2D. Prototype-chain and polymorphic read
   caching.
2. Property-adding put-by-id (`_jit_put_by_id` -> `addNewOwnPropertyInSlot`):
   14% in RayTrace, 10% in Earley-Boyer, ~5% in MiniReact, 3-4% in
   TypeScript. Cached add-property transitions.
3. Native builtin calls (`NativeFunction::_jitCallImpl`): 1-3% everywhere.

## Setup

- Binary: `cmake-build-release` (Release, `HERMESVM_ALLOW_JIT=1`, HV64).
- Every benchmark precompiled: `hermesc -O -emit-binary`, run from .hbc.
- `taskset -c 2`, serialized, 3 reps. `-Xjit=off` vs `-Xjit`.
- Host: ser8, Ryzen 7 8845HS, powersave governor with boost. Ratios are
  the point, not absolute numbers.
- Profiles: `perf record -F 2000`, one run, `-Xjit`. "JIT %" is the share
  of samples in the `[JIT]` DSO; the rest is almost entirely the hermes
  binary (runtime helpers, builtins, GC).

## Reproduce

```
B=cmake-build-release/bin
$B/hermesc -O -emit-binary -out deltablue.hbc benchmarks/octane/deltablue.js
taskset -c 2 $B/hermes -Xjit=off deltablue.hbc
taskset -c 2 $B/hermes -Xjit deltablue.hbc
perf record -F 2000 taskset -c 2 $B/hermes -Xjit deltablue.hbc
perf report --stdio --sort dso; perf report --stdio --sort sym
```
