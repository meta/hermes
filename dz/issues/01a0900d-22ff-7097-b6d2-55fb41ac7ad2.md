A poisoned ByVal site (two observed TA kinds) keeps only the first
kind's tier; the second kind's traffic runs through the demoted
helper forever. For a 50/50 mix that caps the speedup at 1.25x
(Amdahl) -- the GetByVal poisoned benchmarks measure 1.15x/1.17x, and
same-kind alternation measures 1.69x, confirming the untiered half is
the entire gap.

Follow-up: emit tiers for BOTH recorded kinds at a poisoned site
(record the second kind instead of only poisoning; chain kind guards
TA1 -> TA2 -> JSArray -> helper). Estimated ~1.69x on the 50/50 mix.
Costs one more record field and a second tier emission; the monotone
rules extend naturally (never drop either). Applies to put and get.
